Appliance Maintenance Tips & Care Guides

By R.K. Cool Services | Updated 2026

5 Tips to Keep Your AC Cooling Faster

Your air conditioner works hardest during Mumbai's hot and humid months. With a few simple maintenance steps, you can improve cooling efficiency, reduce electricity bills, and extend the life of your AC unit.

  • Clean or replace filters monthly — Clogged filters reduce airflow and force the AC to work harder, consuming more power.
  • Keep the outdoor unit clear — Ensure no dust, leaves, or debris block the condenser fins. Allow at least 2 feet of clearance around it.
  • Schedule regular jet cleaning — Professional jet cleaning removes deep dust buildup from coils and improves cooling by up to 30%.
  • Check for gas leakage — If your AC takes too long to cool or makes unusual sounds, it may need a gas recharge. Call a technician promptly.
  • Set the thermostat to 24°C — This is the optimal temperature for comfort and energy efficiency in Mumbai's climate.
Pro Tip:

Get your AC serviced at least twice a year — before summer starts and after the monsoon. Regular servicing prevents 80% of major breakdowns.

Why is your Refrigerator not cooling?

A warm refrigerator is one of the most common complaints we receive. Here are the most frequent causes:

  • Gas leakage or low refrigerant — The most common cause. Requires professional gas charging to restore cooling.
  • Dirty condenser coils — Dust on the coils prevents heat dissipation. Clean them every 6 months.
  • Faulty compressor — If the fridge hums but doesn't cool, the compressor may need replacement.
  • Blocked air vents — Overpacking the fridge blocks cold air circulation. Keep vents clear.

How to Clean Your Washing Machine Drum

Front-load and top-load washing machines can develop mold and bad odors over time. Here's how to keep your drum fresh:

  • Run an empty hot water cycle with 2 cups of white vinegar once a month.
  • Wipe the rubber gasket after every wash to prevent mold buildup.
  • Leave the door slightly open between washes to allow air circulation.
  • Use the right amount of detergent — excess soap leaves residue that breeds bacteria.

Microwave Safety Tips for Every Home

  • Never use metal containers or aluminum foil in a microwave — they cause sparks and can damage the magnetron.
  • Use only microwave-safe glass or ceramic containers.
  • If the microwave sparks or makes unusual noises, stop using it immediately and call a technician.
  • Keep the interior clean — food splatters can absorb energy and cause hot spots.
