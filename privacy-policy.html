Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

1. Introduction

R.K. Cool Services ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you visit our website or book our home appliance repair services.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following basic information to fulfill your service requests:

  • Contact Information: Your name, phone number, and email address submitted via our booking form.
  • Service Request Details: Type of appliance service needed and your doorstep location details.
  • Technical & Usage Data: Anonymized technical details like device type and browser usage via Google Ads and Google Analytics tracking.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your details are used strictly for service delivery and customer communication:

  • To schedule and confirm your appliance repair booking.
  • To dispatch technicians for doorstep repair and servicing.
  • To communicate status updates or respond to your support queries.
  • To measure website performance and improve service delivery.

4. Data Protection & Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to any third parties. Data is shared only under the following essential operational conditions:

  • With assigned repair technicians solely for reaching your location and completing the service.
  • With secure third-party infrastructure providers (such as Web3Forms for form processing and Google Ads for analytics/conversions).

5. Security of Your Information

We use industry-standard administrative and technical safeguards to keep your contact information secure from unauthorized access or disclosure.

6. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Our website utilizes cookies and performance tracking tags (Google Ads gtag.js) to monitor ad efficiency and user interactions. You can adjust your browser settings to decline or manage cookies at any time.

7. Customer Rights

You reserve the right to review, update, or request the deletion of your personal contact records from our service log by contacting our support team.
