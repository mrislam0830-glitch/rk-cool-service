Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

1. Introduction

R.K. Cool Services ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our website or book our appliance repair services.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Contact Information: Your name, phone number, and email address when you submit a booking form.
  • Service Details: The type of appliance service you request and your address for doorstep service.
  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type, and usage data collected via Google Analytics and Google Ads tracking.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information for the following purposes:

  • To process your service booking and contact you regarding your repair request.
  • To provide doorstep appliance repair services at your location.
  • To improve our website and services based on your feedback.
  • To send promotional offers (only if you have opted in).

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal data. We may share your information with:

  • Our technicians who will visit your address for repair service.
  • Third-party services like Web3Forms (for form submission) and Google (for analytics), as needed to operate our website.

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your personal data from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

6. Cookies

Our website uses cookies for Google Analytics and Google Ads tracking to improve user experience and measure advertising effectiveness. You can disable cookies through your browser settings.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to your personal data.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of your personal data.
  • Opt out of marketing communications at any time.
